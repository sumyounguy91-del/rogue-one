# Sample: Finish-Line Sheet (fictional)

**Not a case study. Not a testimonial. Not evidence of revenue.**
Client name is fictional (“Northline Ops”). No dollar amounts. No performance claims.

This is what a **Finish-Line Critique** deliverable looks like: one stranger-verifiable outcome, not an “unlimited AI assistant.”

## 1. Finish line (one sentence)
Northline sells a **closed AP exception packet**: when an invoice exception is resolved and the ERP status is updated, the job is done — not “an AI AP assistant.”

## 2. In scope / out of scope
| In | Out |
|----|-----|
| Triage exception → draft resolution note → propose ERP field updates | Full month-close automation |
| Human approve before any ERP write | Autonomous payments |
| Slack notify AP lead when packet ready | Replacing the AP team |
| Audit log of agent steps for that packet | Training on the customer’s full chart of accounts |

## 3. Success check (stranger-verifiable)
A packet is **done** when: (a) exception ID has a resolution note, (b) proposed ERP fields are listed, (c) AP lead clicked Approve or Reject in Slack within the trial window, (d) agent stopped — no further writes.

## 4. Pricing
**Not included here.** Structure belongs to the operator and buyer. This sample intentionally has **no numbers** so nothing looks like a fabricated case study.

## 5. Presence cut (buyer owns posting)
**Short:** Stop selling an AP assistant. Sell a closed exception packet — note, fields, human approve, stop.
**Longer:** Soft consensus is “AI does AP.” The quieter product is one finish line: a packet a stranger can check. Magic demos get likes; closed packets get renewals.

## 6. Refuse list (claims to stop this week)
- “Autonomous AP” without a human approve step
- Guaranteed % cost savings without a measured baseline the buyer owns
- “Works with any ERP” without a named connector list
- Unlimited seats as the hero metric

## 7. Delivery checklist
- [ ] Finish-Line Sheet (this)
- [ ] Pricing sketch (numbers only after operator + buyer agree — not public)
- [ ] Presence cut
- [ ] Refuse list
- [ ] Short debrief notes
